RESEARCH

Research

We have studied a wide range of laser processing technologies — macro processing such as heat treatment, welding and cladding, ultra-precision laser machining, and dual-beam pulsed laser deposition. Our current focus is advancing these technologies with artificial intelligence, while training researchers ready for the AI era of manufacturing.

CURRENT FOCUS

AI for Laser Materials Processing

01

Real-time process monitoring

Deep learning models read thermal and coaxial camera images during processing to predict what cannot be measured directly: laser beam absorptance in keyhole welding, top and bottom bead widths, and cross-sectional hardness and phase distributions in laser heat treatment.

02

Generative surrogate models

GAN-based and image-to-image models trained on simulation and synthetic data replace expensive multiphysics computations — residual stress and melt pool prediction, electrodynamic beam reflection, and full-field deformation in line heating.

03

Vision-to-code autonomous processing

Models that turn camera images directly into machine control code, enabling automated weld seam tracking, AI-assisted femtosecond laser patterning, and narrow-gap butt welding without explicit geometric reconstruction.

RESEARCH TOPICS

Research Topics

Deep learning for laser hardening prediction

A deep learning model predicts the hardness distribution over the cross-section of carbon steel after laser hardening, outperforming our earlier model based on carbon diffusion time.

Laser heat treatmentDeep learningHardness prediction

Deep learning for laser welding prediction

From laser process parameters, a deep learning model predicts the cross-sectional shape of the weld bead, opening the way to data-driven design of welding conditions.

Keyhole weldingBead geometryDeep learning

Femtosecond laser surface patterning (LIPSS)

Laser-induced periodic surface structures and their modified forms let us control the reflectance of metal surfaces, producing fine decorative patterns. Femtosecond patterning combined with wet etching also creates surfaces with tunable wetting behavior.

Femtosecond laserLIPSSSurface functionalization

Electromagnetic analysis of laser processing

We solve Maxwell's equations numerically to model how a laser beam propagates and interacts with materials — for example, femtosecond laser interaction with silicon using electromagnetic analysis coupled with a two-temperature model and an ionization model.

FDTDLaser–matter interactionTwo-temperature model

Dual-beam pulsed laser deposition for broadband antireflection coatings

By co-depositing glass and silicon on silicon with a mixing ratio that changes continuously along a designed profile (e.g., linear or Southwell), we fabricate graded-index coatings that increase light absorption over a broad wavelength range for solar cells.

Pulsed laser depositionFunctionally graded filmsSolar cells

Fluid–structure interaction for residual stress analysis

Most studies ignore the interaction between liquid and solid across the mushy zone during melting and solidification. We treat it as a fluid–structure interaction problem with a unified momentum equation approach to compute thermal residual stresses more faithfully.

Residual stressFluid–structure interactionComputational modeling
